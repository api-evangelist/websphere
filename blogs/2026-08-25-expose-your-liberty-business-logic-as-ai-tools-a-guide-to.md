What is MCP? Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that enables AI applications to interact with and utilise external systems. The mcpServer-1.0 feature for Open Liberty allows developers to expose the business logic of their applications, making it discoverable, understandable, and invocable by AI applications.
